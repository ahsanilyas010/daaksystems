How It Works

From Brief to Deployed

A focused delivery process for enterprise software projects.

01
Discovery & Scope
We map out your business flows, data model, and user roles before writing a single line of code. A detailed SRS document is produced and approved before development begins.
02
Design & Prototype
Figma wireframes for every screen. You see exactly what the system will look like and how it will behave before development starts — reducing change requests later.
03
Build & Test
Iterative sprints with bi-weekly demos. Full-stack Next.js with PostgreSQL. Role-based access control, audit logs, and seed data included. No half-baked handoffs.
04
Deploy & Support
Production deployment on Vercel or your preferred cloud provider. Handover documentation, training session, and 60-day post-launch support included.
